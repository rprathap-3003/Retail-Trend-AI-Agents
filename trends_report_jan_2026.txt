==================================================
   SUIT TRENDS (14 items)
==================================================

 1. Double-Breasted Revival
    Colors: navy, charcoal, grey, black
    Style: The classic 6x2 double-breasted suit returns, combining timeless elegance with a refined, contemporary silhouette.
    Pattern: Primarily solid colors to emphasize the cut, but also classic pinstripes.
----------------------------------------

 2. The New Navy Classic
    Colors: navy, midnight blue, royal blue
    Style: Versatile navy suits are replacing traditional black as the new go-to classic for formal events like prom.
    Pattern: Solid color is the most prominent style for this trend.
----------------------------------------

 3. Emerald Green Suit
    Colors: emerald, deep green, forest green
    Style: Vibrant emerald green suits offer a fresh and bold statement, especially for weddings and special occasions.
    Pattern: Solid color, often in rich fabrics like velvet or satin.
----------------------------------------

 4. Rich Aubergine Suit
    Colors: aubergine, deep purple, plum
    Style: Deep, sophisticated aubergine-colored suits are a key trend for making a personal and bold style statement.
    Pattern: Most impactful as a solid color, often seen in three-piece configurations.
----------------------------------------

 5. Refined Jacquard Suit
    Colors: charcoal, silver, deep blue, black
    Style: Suits featuring intricate jacquard weaves provide understated texture and elevated design that is detailed up close.
    Pattern: Intricate, woven, often tone-on-tone floral or geometric patterns.
----------------------------------------

 6. Lightweight Linen Ensemble
    Colors: beige, white, light blue, tan
    Style: Prioritizing comfort and a laid-back luxury feel, linen suits are a top choice for outdoor and garden weddings.
    Pattern: Solid color with a natural slub texture inherent to the fabric.
----------------------------------------

 7. The Standalone Vest
    Colors: grey, navy, brown, beige
    Style: The vest returns in full force as a key layering piece, worn on its own over a shirt or as part of a suit.
    Pattern: Solid, tweed, check, or pinstripe patterns are all relevant.
----------------------------------------

 8. Relaxed Fit Tailoring
    Colors: earth tones, grey, beige, olive
    Style: Moving away from rigid fits, suiting embraces more relaxed, comfortable, and flexible silhouettes for modern work.
    Pattern: Typically solid colors or soft, subtle textures to enhance the comfortable aesthetic.
----------------------------------------

 9. The Modern White Tuxedo
    Colors: white, off-white, cream
    Style: A stylish white tuxedo offers a sharp, fresh, and vibrant alternative for formal events and weddings.
    Pattern: Solid, often with contrasting black or navy lapels for a classic look.
----------------------------------------

10. Bold Pattern Statement
    Colors: black, white, brown, multi-color
    Style: Loud, expressive, and bold patterns are being used to make a maximalist statement in modern suiting.
    Pattern: Animal prints like zebra or cow print, and other loud, graphic patterns.
----------------------------------------

11. All-Over Stripes
    Colors: navy with white, grey with white, black with white
    Style: Classic stripes are appearing on everything, bringing a retro yet sharp feel back to suiting and menswear.
    Pattern: Pinstripes and chalk stripes of varying widths.
----------------------------------------

12. Textured Material Focus
    Colors: brown, tan, black, grey
    Style: Unique and tactile fabrics like spotty suedes and hairy panels are being used to add depth and a Western-revival feel.
    Pattern: Textured solids, spotty patterns, or panels with a 'hairy' texture.
----------------------------------------

13. The Three-Piece Comeback
    Colors: aubergine, navy, charcoal, grey
    Style: The classic three-piece suit is trending for formalwear, offering a complete and sophisticated look with a matching vest.
    Pattern: Solid, pinstripe, or check patterns are popular for this style.
----------------------------------------

14. Sharp Cuts & Modern Details
    Colors: navy, black, grey, charcoal
    Style: A focus on sharp cuts and modern detailing defines the new era of suiting, emphasizing a deliberate and well-put-together look.
    Pattern: Solid colors are preferred to highlight the precise tailoring and silhouette.
----------------------------------------

==================================================
   SHOES TRENDS (2 items)
==================================================

 1. Animal Texture Footwear
    Colors: brown, black, white, tan
    Style: Shoes featuring animal-inspired textures like faux snakeskin, pony hair, and cow print are a key trend.
    Pattern: Faux snakeskin, cow print, zebra stripes, and pony hair textures.
----------------------------------------

 2. Gorpcore Sneakers
    Colors: earth tones, black, grey, bright accents
    Style: Trail and hiking-inspired sneakers, or 'gorpcore' shoes, remain a mainstream footwear choice, even paired with relaxed tailoring.
    Pattern: Multi-panel, technical designs with rugged soles.
----------------------------------------

==================================================
   TIE TRENDS (2 items)
==================================================

 1. Patterned Silk Tie
    Colors: burgundy, navy, forest green, gold
    Style: Ties made from silk fabrics featuring classic paisley patterns are a staple for sophisticated professional menswear.
    Pattern: Intricate and colorful paisley patterns.
----------------------------------------

 2. Textured Grenadine Tie
    Colors: navy, burgundy, black, silver
    Style: Grenadine ties are trending for their unique, crisp, and textured weave, adding subtle sophistication to an outfit.
    Pattern: A distinctive open-weave texture known as grenadine (garza fina or garza grossa).
----------------------------------------

==================================================
   ACCESSORY TRENDS (4 items)
==================================================

 1. The Statement Brooch
    Colors: gold, silver, enamel colors
    Style: Driven by a surge in interest, brooches are being worn on suit lapels as a key statement accessory.
    Pattern: Varies from abstract shapes to floral, animal, or heirloom-style motifs.
----------------------------------------

 2. Tie Accessories Return
    Colors: silver, gold, gunmetal, rose gold
    Style: Classic tie accessories like tie clips and cufflinks are making a serious comeback, adding a polished detail to suiting.
    Pattern: Minimalist bars, engraved details, or small decorative elements.
----------------------------------------

 3. Maximalist Accessorizing
    Colors: gold, silver, bright colors, mixed metals
    Style: A 'more is more' approach to accessories is trending, encouraging playful and bold combinations of jewelry and accents.
    Pattern: Layered and mixed patterns, textures, and styles across different pieces.
----------------------------------------

 4. Heirloom-Inspired Jewelry
    Colors: aged gold, tarnished silver, gemstones
    Style: Men are incorporating heirloom or vintage-style jewelry into their looks, adding a personal and timeless touch.
    Pattern: Ornate, classic, or antique designs on rings, cufflinks, and necklaces.
----------------------------------------
